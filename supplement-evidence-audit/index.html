Supplement Evidence Audit

121 consumer-supplement compounds across 12 outcome categories, graded 1–5 on the quality of industry-independent human-trial evidence — not popularity. Click any row for the full reasoning. Compiled 2026-08-04.

Of 121 compounds, 8 score ≥4 and only five are distinct molecules — creatine, caffeine, whey/protein, psyllium fibre, and iron (the last only if deficient). 75 sit at 2–2.5: a real mechanism, a plausible meta-analysis, and an effect that doesn’t clear the MCID, hinges on industry funding or one outlier trial, or vanishes outside the deficient population it was studied in. That band is where most supplement money goes. The only unconditional 5 is creatine for muscle; the whole longevity aisle (NAD, senolytics) tops out at 2.5 with no human outcome, only biomarkers.

What actually earns a place

Tier A — buy, evidence is real (≥4):

  • Creatine monohydrate (5) — the best-evidenced sports supplement; 3–5 g/day.
  • Caffeine (4.5) — the only “energy” agent that works; you already get this from matcha.
  • Whey / protein powder (4.5) — only if you’re not already hitting your protein target from food.
  • Psyllium husk (4) — guideline-grade fibre for IBS / constipation; needs ≥10 g/day.
  • Iron (4) — only if ferritin is low; otherwise a 1.

Tier B — only for a specific, named problem (3–3.5): melatonin for circadian problems, not insomnia; S. boulardii / PHGG for gut complaints; zinc acetate/gluconate lozenges at cold onset; nicotinamide 500 mg ×2 for high skin-cancer risk; Polypodium as a sunscreen adjunct; beta-alanine for 1–4 min efforts; collagen+vitC pre-load on top of tendon loading; curcumin (Meriva) for knee OA; bacopa (12 wk); vitamin D if deficient; calcium+D for fracture risk. Everything else — the 2s — don’t buy.

Where the real fix is not a supplement

  • Patellar / tendinopathy → heavy-slow resistance loading. Collagen+vitC pre-load is an adjunct, nothing without the loading.
  • Chronic insomnia → CBT-I. Melatonin is for circadian problems at a low 0.5–1 mg dose, not the 5–10 mg most products sell.
  • Knee osteoarthritis → exercise therapy + weight management beat every joint supplement here.
  • Low energy without a deficiency → sleep, training, calories. Adaptogen “energy” blends are a 1.
  • Longevity → exercise and not over-eating. No NAD booster or senolytic has a human outcome.

Method. 67-agent adversarial workflow: one skeptical researcher per category re-derived every grade from primary trials; 50 high-stakes/compromised/moved grades were handed to an independent reviewer told to refute them (it trimmed six downward, four of which failed outright, and nudged two up). Seven checks applied to each compound: funding-source subgroup, outlier leverage, effect-size vs MCID, trial vs retail dose, population transfer, dose–response direction, regulatory advisories.

Caveats & not medical advice. Grades measure evidence quality, not whether a compound works for you. Product links are Amazon search URLs chosen for form and dose — confirm the per-serving active/elemental dose against the trial dose before buying. Supplement potency is loosely regulated.
