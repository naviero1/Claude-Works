# How the World Uses AI — Global Usage Report (2025–2026)

A self-contained, data-driven research report on how people around the world use
generative AI, segmented across four lenses: **country/region**, **socioeconomic
class**, **occupation/use-case**, and **demographics** (age, gender, education).

## Files

- **`index.html`** — the full report. Open it in any browser. All charts and
  infographics are rendered with inline SVG/CSS — **no internet connection or
  external libraries required**.
- **`data.json`** — every figure used in the report, with its source and date,
  for transparency and reuse.

## What it covers

The report focuses on **usage mechanics** with hard, chart-ready numbers:

1. **Scale** — adoption speed and reach (700M+ ChatGPT weekly users, ~10% of
   adults, 1B+ monthly genAI users, 53% adoption in 3 years).
2. **Tools** — market/traffic share (ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude,
   Perplexity) and the year-over-year shift.
3. **Modalities** — text vs image vs code vs data, and the "coding paradox"
   between ChatGPT and Claude user bases.
4. **Prompts & tasks** — top conversation topics, message intent
   (asking/doing/expressing), and Claude's technical-work skew.
5. **Class & the digital divide** — per-capita usage by income, Global
   North–South gap.
6. **Occupation & use-case** — developers, office workers, students,
   enterprises (consumer vs API, augmentation vs automation).
7. **Demographics** — steep age gradient, the closed gender gap, the youth tilt.
8. **Geography** — absolute volume vs per-capita leaders, regional challengers.

## Method & caveats

Built from a multi-source, adversarially fact-checked research pass: 27 sources
fetched, 114 claims extracted, top 25 put through 3-vote verification (23
confirmed). Core figures are first-party vendor usage data (OpenAI, Anthropic),
independent surveys (Pew, Ipsos, Reuters Institute, Stanford HAI, Microsoft,
BCG), and traffic estimates (Similarweb).

**Read the numbers carefully.** They are dated 2025–2026 snapshots that move
fast; vendor data is self-reported and platform-specific (Claude skews to
developers, ChatGPT is mainstream — neither alone equals "how everyone uses
AI"); gender is name-inferred; Similarweb figures are modeled web traffic only.
Full caveats and a tagged source list are in the report itself.
