## Signal-strength bands

- **Hot (11)** — active FDA enforcement or a public quality/safety crisis (Warning Letter, Form 483, Class I recall, CRL citing CMC). Fastest demand: remediation + mock re-inspection now, usually with a QA-resourcing surge.
- **Warm (15)** — approaching or just past a regulatory milestone (first approval, first BLA, PDUFA, new device clearance). PAI-readiness window.
- **Developing (13)** — new facility, expansion, tech transfer, QA hiring surge, or M&A integration. QMS build-out on a longer sales cycle.

Sales-research intelligence compiled from public sources (FDA enforcement pages, company IR/press releases, trade and regional press) as of **August 2026**, for prospecting only.
