### Credentials worth knowing about in this domain

- **CSQP — Certified Supplier Quality Professional** (ASQ (American Society for Quality)) — The one credential that matches his exact job title, and its body of knowledge maps almost one-to-one onto this module. Critically for him, ASQ certifications require no degree: CSQP asks for 8 years of on-the-job experience (3 in a decision-making role), and a degree only waives years of experience — his 20 years qualify outright. It converts two decades of adjacent vendor work into a recognized SQE signal.
- **CQA — Certified Quality Auditor** (ASQ (American Society for Quality)) — Targets his single biggest formal gap (structured auditing). Same experience-based eligibility as CSQP — 8 years experience, no degree required, education only substitutes for experience years — so he is fully eligible. Recognized across manufacturing, biotech, and regulated industries, making it strong transfer value beyond the current employer.
- **CQE — Certified Quality Engineer** (ASQ (American Society for Quality)) — The broadest-market quality credential, covering MSA, acceptance sampling, SPC and reliability — the areas where his Minitab/statistics strength gives him a head start most candidates lack. Eligibility is 8 years experience with no degree requirement (degree only waives years), so his lack of a bachelor's is no barrier. Best pursued after CSQP/CQA since it is the hardest exam of the three.
- **ISO 9001:2015 Lead Auditor (certified training course)** (Exemplar Global- or CQI/IRCA-accredited training providers) — A one-week (roughly 40-hour) course with exam and no degree or prerequisite requirements — the fastest credential on this list. Immediately usable: it licenses him, in practice, to design and run the supplier audits his company currently has no one qualified to perform, and it pairs naturally with the ASQ CQA for on-paper audit credibility.
