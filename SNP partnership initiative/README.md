# SNP partnership initiative

**Purpose.** Turn our sole-source relationship with **SNP Inc. (Durham, NC)** — supplier of the liquid polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) solution (P/N 666438, ~11% solids, 55-gal drums of 450 lb, 18-day shelf life) — into a deliberate strategic partnership, and explore whether SNP's other hydrogel lines (sodium alginate, guar gum) can serve other projects and grow the business we do together.

**Why now.** The second-source work (see `../PVA second supplier initiative/`) produced one qualified-capable alternative (PCI Manufacturing) at $3.50/lb versus SNP's $0.75/lb. The leadership recommendation is Option 3: a strengthened relationship with SNP plus one or two 5-drum "keep-alive" batches a year from PCI (Option 1 = second supplier at one-third; Option 2 = strengthen SNP only, no volume moved). The next conversation with SNP is where that strategy becomes real, so the partnership agenda needs to be designed before it — not discovered in it.

**Owner.** Oscar Penny, Supply Chain.
