## Per-match notes (player + cohesion layer)

1. **South Africa vs Canada** — Canada's host status + a stronger spine (Jonathan David as
   disruptor) makes them the prior favorite; South Africa's young, cohesive side is exactly
   the kind of disciplined unit that drags this to a tight 1–1 / penalties. *Upset path:* SA
   stay compact, nick a set-piece.
2. **Brazil vs Japan** — Class gap on paper, but Japan are the tournament's most cohesive
   high-press unit (drew Netherlands 2–2). Brazil's front line wins it more often than not;
   Japan is the live upset of the round. *Uncertainty:* Brazil's defensive concentration.
3. **Germany vs Paraguay** — Over-rating trap flagged: Germany's 7–1 then loss to Ecuador
   says don't trust the goal count. Paraguay's defensive cohesion (0–0 vs Australia) keeps
   this low; Germany's individual quality should still tell. Penalties are a real out for
   Paraguay.
4. **Mexico vs Ecuador** — The altitude game: **Estadio Azteca (~2,240 m)** + host crowd is a
   genuine λ-booster for Mexico, but Ecuador are organized and just beat Germany. Low-scoring
   coin-flip; Mexico edges on context. *Strong upset candidate.*
5. **Netherlands vs Morocco** — Marquee toss-up. Morocco are the 2022 cohesion blueprint
   (elite shape beating bigger names, Hakimi as disruptor); the Netherlands have more
   individual talent but a leaky look (2–2 vs Japan). Genuinely 50/50 → penalties very live.
6. **Côte d'Ivoire vs Norway** — Haaland (+ Ødegaard) is the disruptor that tilts a close
   game; Côte d'Ivoire's athleticism keeps it competitive but Norway's ceiling is higher.
7. **France vs Sweden** — Clear class gap; Mbappé in form. Sweden's 5–1 vs Tunisia is noise
   against this level of opponent. France comfortable barring an early upset.
8. **Belgium vs South Korea** — Belgium's 5–1 flatters an inconsistent group (0–0 Iran,
   1–1 Egypt). South Korea are cohesive and fast with Son as a transition disruptor — a
   classic banana-skin. Lower confidence than the market lean suggests.
9. **USA vs Bosnia** — Home crowd is worth real λ, but the 3–2 loss to Türkiye exposed
   fragility; Bosnia's Džeko is a veteran disruptor. USA favored but volatile.
10. **England vs Senegal** — England's depth vs Senegal's pace, power and cohesion (5–0 vs
    Iraq; a proven knockout side). England should have enough, but Senegal is the most
    dangerous "third-placed" team in the bracket.
11. **Spain vs Austria** — Spain's possession control + Yamal disruptor vs an organized but
    outclassed Austria. Tight first half, Spain pulls clear.
12. **Switzerland vs Iran** — The cohesion benchmark: Iran's ultra-organized block (0–0 vs
    Belgium) drags this toward 1–0 / 1–1 and penalties. Switzerland favored but this is the
    round's most likely low-scoring stalemate.
13. **Portugal vs Ghana** — Portugal's attacking depth vs a leaky Ghana defense; expect goals
    and a comfortable margin barring a Ghana smash-and-grab.
14. **Australia vs Egypt** — True coin-flip. Salah is the single biggest disruptor on the
    pitch; Australia's organization and set-pieces keep it close. Edge Egypt on Salah.
15. **Argentina vs Cabo Verde** — Holders vs the cinderella. Cape Verde's cohesion earns
    respect and likely keeps the score down, but the talent gap (Messi/Álvarez) is decisive.
16. **Colombia vs Croatia** — Colombia in form with James pulling strings; Croatia's
    tournament-savvy veteran core (Modrić) is the upset vehicle but looks a step slow this
    cycle (lost to England). Colombia favored.

## v1.1 — Player & availability adjustments (after deep player research)

The player/disruptor + cohesion research (see [`08-player-ratings.md`](08-player-ratings.md))
moves several lines. The biggest deltas:

- **Brazil–Japan** → *closer than the market.* Brazil are materially weakened (**Raphinha OUT**;
  Neymar/Rodrygo/Estêvão unavailable) — Vinícius Jr is now the lone elite weapon. But Japan also
  lose **Mitoma + Minamino** (and Endo is doubtful), thinning their creativity. Net: still Brazil
  (2–1) but lower confidence; Japan's cohesion makes the upset price likely too long.
- **Portugal–Ghana** → *upgrade Portugal.* **Kudus + Salisu OUT** strips Ghana's best player and a
  defender. Revise to **3–0**, confidence High.
- **South Africa–Canada** → *upgrade Canada.* SA lose all-time top scorer **Zwane (suspended)**;
  Canada have an in-form Jonathan David (group hat-trick). Hold **Canada 2–1**, nudge confidence up
  to Medium (watch Davies' fitness).
- **Germany–Paraguay** → *slight Germany upgrade.* Paraguay lose midfield goal threat **Diego Gómez
  (suspended)**; Germany still without Schlotterbeck. Hold **2–1**.
- **Netherlands–Morocco** → *upset even more live.* The Dutch start a makeshift CB pairing
  (**Timber + de Ligt both OUT**) against Hakimi/Brahim and a shootout-proof keeper in Bono. True
  coin-flip; lean Morocco on **draw-then-penalties**.
- **Mexico–Ecuador** → *keeps the coin-flip.* Mexico's first-choice GK **Malagón is OUT** (39-yo
  Ochoa in goal) — a real vulnerability that offsets the Azteca-altitude home edge. Ecuador have
  Caicedo (control) + Plata (scored vs Germany, Libertadores-tested). Hold **1–1, Mexico narrow.**
- **France–Sweden** → *France even safer.* Sweden lose CB **Hien** against Mbappé/Dembélé. Hold **3–1**.
- **Switzerland–Iran** → *lowest-scoring game on the board.* Iran's low block + **Taremi (Olympiacos)**
  on the counter; Azmoun omitted. Favor the **under**; Switzerland 1–0 or pens.
- **Colombia–Croatia** → **Luis Díaz** (not James) is Colombia's swing factor; James (34, MLS) is the
  set-piece architect. Croatia's core looks a step slow. Hold **Colombia 2–1**.
- **Australia–Egypt** → **Salah** is the difference-maker in a coin-flip; hold **Egypt 1–2**.

> Net bracket effect: Brazil and Netherlands are the two "favorites" whose paths got harder; Portugal
> and Canada got easier. Argentina, France and Spain remain the cleanest favorites in R32.
