# Staking & Allocation — June 27, 2026

How to *size* bets across today's slate, not just pick directions. Allocation is where most
bettors lose: good reads + bad sizing = a losing bankroll.

> Research/analysis, not financial advice. Stake only what you can afford to lose; the
> disciplined play is frequently **no bet**. Markets settle on 90'+stoppage (not ET/pens).

## The framework

**1. Unit system.** 1 unit = **1% of bankroll**. Think in units, never dollars.

**2. Edge is measured vs the de-vigged market, not vs your gut.**
`edge = your_probability − market_de-vigged_probability`. If your number equals the market's,
your edge is **0** and the correct stake is **0** — taking the market price just pays the vig.

**3. Fractional Kelly (¼ Kelly) for sizing, capped.**
`stake (units) = 0.25 × (edge × decimal_odds − (1 − ... )) ...` → in practice use the tiers below.
- **Strong value** (edge ≥ +7% vs market, decent odds): 2–3 units
- **Moderate value** (edge +3–7%): 1–1.5 units
- **Directional lean, no edge over market** (you just agree with the price): 0–0.5 unit "for action"
- **No edge / uncertain / unanchored**: **0 units (pass)**

**4. Hard caps.** Max **5% of bankroll** total exposure across a single day's slate. Max **2.5%**
on any one game. These caps are the difference between a bad day and a blown bankroll.

**5. Avoid by construction.**
- Heavy favorites at tiny odds (Argentina/England to win at ~1.1–1.2) — minimal return, real ruin risk.
- Big handicaps on rotation-risk games (Argentina −1.5/−2.5 when they may rest starters).
- Stacking correlated legs and calling it diversification (see parlays below).

### Parlay / same-game note
- **Colombia–Portugal Over 2.5 + BTTS Yes are positively correlated** (goals → both score). A
  same-game parlay is reasonable, but the combined price already bakes in the correlation — treat
  the SGP as **one ~1-unit position**, don't also stake each leg.
- Do **not** cross-parlay independent games to manufacture odds; it multiplies vig and variance.
