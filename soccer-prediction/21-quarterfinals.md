### Four lessons (baked into the QF numbers below)
1. **We under-rate organized underdogs and over-rate favorite dominance.** Egypt (16%) led Argentina
   2-0; Switzerland (a low block) knocked out the "stronger" Colombia. Shade favorites DOWN, especially
   vs disciplined low blocks.
2. **Goals are bimodal — match λ to the profiles, don't apply blanket caution.** Attacking clashes are
   goal-fests (Arg-Egy 5, USA-Bel 5, Eng-Mex 5); organized low blocks are 0-0/1-0 (Swi-Col, Spa-Por).
   Our Argentina goals-up revision was right — keep pushing goals when both teams attack.
3. **Elite keepers win shootouts — weight it harder.** Sommer just decided a tie exactly as flagged.
   When a draw is likely and one side has a shootout keeper (Bono, Sommer, Courtois), shade *their*
   advance % UP beyond the generic 50/50.
4. **Flair can be neutralized by organization.** Colombia's Luis Díaz went quiet vs a drilled Swiss
   block — disruptors don't always fire; don't assume they will.

### Team-specific takeaways (relevant to the QF)
- **Argentina:** NOT dominant — dragged to the wire twice (Cape Verde AET, Egypt comeback) and now
  **fatigued**. Beatable, nervy, reliant on Messi bailing them out. Vulnerable to low blocks.
- **Switzerland:** ultra-organized, low-scoring, **Sommer wins shootouts** — a grind-it-out machine.
- **France, Spain, England, Norway, Belgium, Morocco** advance (QF field).
