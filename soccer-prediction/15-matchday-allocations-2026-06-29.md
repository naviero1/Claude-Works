**Why I diverge (defensible):** Netherlands are missing **both** first-choice center-backs (Timber +
de Ligt OUT) — a makeshift back line in front of an aging Van Dijk. Morocco are the **2022 cohesion
blueprint** (Hakimi's pace at the patched CBs, Brahim Díaz between the lines) with **Bono**, a proven

Dutch *brand*; it's slow to price the team sheet. **Disruption edge:** NED attack (Gakpo 9, De Jong 8)
vs Morocco's organized block roughly cancels; Morocco's edge is **defensive cohesion + Bono in a
shootout** against NED's compromised spine. Net: Morocco far more live than 38%.
