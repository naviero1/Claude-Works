4. What actually shows edge (vs what's just picking favorites)

  • Two market-beating value fades won: Morocco (mkt 38% → we said 47%) and Paraguay (mkt 15% → 24%) both advanced. This is the clearest edge — beating the de-vigged market, not the bookie's favorite.
  • Goals revisions 2/2 — both Oscar-prompted; raising λ on attacking-team clashes hit both times.
  • Exact top-3 scoreline hits in tight games: Brazil 2-1, Spain 2-1, England 2-1, Argentina 2-1 — the model reads knockout scorelines well.
  • Coin-flips called right: Egypt (54%), England×2, plus the "grind → penalties" scenario on Switzerland–Colombia (right scenario, wrong side).

5. Honest weaknesses

  • Under-rating organized/beatable underdogs: Egypt led Argentina 2-0 (we gave Egypt 16%); Switzerland beat Colombia (we picked Colombia). Our favorites were too dominant on paper.
  • Under-shooting goals until pushed — the model's default knockout caution was too low twice.
  • Exact scores are hard — 0/6 in the group slate; the value is in the distribution, not a single score.
  • Group-stage / seeding games were our worst (−2u), especially over-backing Over/BTTS in dead-rubbers (Colombia–Portugal 0-0).
  • The advance metric flatters us — favorites usually win; strip that out and the real, repeatable edge is the market-beating fades + the goals reads.
