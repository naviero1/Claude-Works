### 3. Caveats leadership must hear

1. Small-pack prices carry retail margin, a bottle or pail, and often a hazmat or ground-only surcharge (Film #10 is one-third ethanol, flash point 76 F). Shipping is excluded on every LOW and COMPETITIVE row except Fiberglass Warehouse. The same product falls from $2.84 (gal) to $2.49 (case) to $2.32 (20 gal) at one vendor, so the LOW tier is a packaging-and-margin price, not a chemistry price.
2. The HIGH tier and the offshore drum band are ex-works India or FOB China. Product pages were bot-gated, solids are stated on only one listing (Padma, 10-20%), one is partially hydrolyzed (Bhavna, 87-89%), and none show a CoA, TSCA/REACH status or lot traceability. Ocean freight, duty, drums or totes and import handling would add a material amount per pound before any of it reached our dock; that adder was not observed and is not built into the numbers.
3. Solids differ: Film #10 class is 7-8% PVA plus ethanol; Coverall is 11-13% aqueous (the only product in the set that is genuinely the same class as our ~11% aqueous solution); One-Coat is "2-3x standard" with no MSDS figure; lab solutions are 3-5%; offshore solids are unstated. Per-pound-of-solution comparisons flatter dilute products.
4. Freight basis is mixed: SNP $0.75 is delivered, CJB $8.42 is landed, PCI basis is as quoted, and nearly every market row excludes shipping. The gap between market and SNP is therefore understated, not overstated.
5. None of these products carry medical-device supplier controls (ISO 13485 or equivalent quality agreement, change notification, lot CoA, retained samples). Composites release agents contain dyes, surfactants and defoamers and are not a super-hydrolyzed, high-MW Selvol S-1551F-D solution; the Rexco Coverall hydrolysis grade is unknown. These are price comparables, not qualified alternates.
6. Evidence depth is thin at the quantities that matter: US drum-scale is inferred from one 20-gal lot and six 4-5 gal trade packs, and US tote-scale does not exist publicly. Three rows are PLAUSIBLE rather than CONFIRMED (Merritt Supply, TAP Plastics, Blackburn Marine on pack size); none of them drives a tier value. All prices were read on 2026-09-28.

### 4. What the market says about each quote

SNP at $0.75/lb delivered is below every US-listed pre-mixed PVA solution at any quantity (the cheapest US price found anywhere is $2.20/lb for a Coverall trade case, and the largest US lot is $2.32/lb), below the ton-scale FOB Asia median of $1.01/lb before freight and duty, and level with the median of the specified Indian ex-works drum listings ($0.76/lb). It is an offshore-bulk price for a US-delivered, custom-grade drum product; the public market does not offer a way to replicate it at drum quantity.
