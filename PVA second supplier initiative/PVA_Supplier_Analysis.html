The constraint

One hard gate decides who qualifies

Deal-breaker

Hold a full batch at 90–95 °C (194–203 °F) for 30–45 min under agitation. Super-hydrolyzed high-MW PVA won't dissolve otherwise. Confirmed by our 5TT-11A bench process and the Sekisui/Kuraray guides. This single requirement eliminated ArroChem (85 °C ceiling) and ILC Dover, and it's the first question for every candidate.

Economics

What a second source costs

The “redundancy premium” is the extra you pay over the all-SNP baseline. Three levers drive it: how much volume the second source gets, how much more it charges than SNP, and whether SNP raises its price on the volume it keeps. Capped at 1 drum/week (~35%).

Key insight

An SNP price increase lands on the larger retained volume (~65%), so it's often a bigger driver of total cost than the second-source premium itself. At price parity with SNP holding, the cost is ≈ $0 — the ideal but unlikely floor.

Cadence

Order rhythm is set by the 18-day shelf life

Made fresh, no stockpiling. At ~1 drum/week you can hold at most ~2 drums before the oldest ages out — which rules out large batches.

Weekly · 1 drum/wk Recommended

  • Matches the 1-drum/week cap
  • Freshest; well within shelf life
  • APV does small batches (from 5 gal) → single drum is fine

Biweekly · 2 drums/2 wks Optional

  • Still ~1/wk average & within shelf life
  • Means holding 2 drums at once
  • Marginally fewer setups — minor price help

3+ drums / batch Avoid

  • Exceeds the 1-drum/week cap
  • 3+ weeks supply > 18-day shelf life
  • Oldest drum ages out → waste / risk

Other factors

What else shapes the decision

Low-volume leverage

This is a small, bottleneck account — weak price leverage. Two qualified, interested suppliers (CJB + APV) is the real lever: it disciplines pricing and protects continuity.

Business retention

Sell the relationship, not the volume — qualified, recurring, blue-chip, low-touch, room to grow. Use the Intuitive name for credibility; set expectations at the division level.

Quality & regulatory

FDA QMSR is in force (2026-02-02): supplier controls follow ISO 13485 §7.4, built on GHTF/SG3/N17, with a Supplier Quality Agreement (change-notification + right-to-audit).

The gating unknown

Viscosity target & tolerance still pending from SNP CoA history — it blocks any firm quote. Suppliers can quote against the 10–12% solids spec meanwhile.
