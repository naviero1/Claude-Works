# Stock Evaluation: 30 Robinhood-Available Stocks by Volatility Tier

**Prepared:** August 5, 2026 (supersedes nothing — companion to `portfolio-recommendation.md` v2)
**Method:** Screened across sectors for quality (real earnings growth, durable competitive position, balance-sheet strength), then verified each candidate against August 2026 market conditions, Q2 earnings season results, and current geopolitical events. Stocks that failed verification were dropped (see "Dropped after verification" at the bottom). All tickers trade on NYSE/Nasdaq and are available on Robinhood.

> **Disclaimer:** Educational research, not personalized financial advice. Betas are approximations and drift over time. Individual stocks — even "low volatility" ones — carry company-specific risk that diversified ETFs do not. Position sizes matter more than picks: no single stock should exceed ~3–5% of your total portfolio.

## The world as of August 5, 2026 (what the lists are calibrated to)

- **Markets at records:** S&P 500 ~7,737 (+~10% YTD), Dow 54,086, Nasdaq 26,585 after a two-day surge.
- **Iran de-escalation:** Trump called off planned strikes; US, Iran and Oman are close to a 60-day interim deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. Oil has tumbled to ~$75.7/bbl from its conflict highs. This is disinflationary and bullish for risk assets — but the deal is interim and could break.
- **Fed risk runs the other way:** funds rate 3.50–3.75%, June CPI 3.5% (down from May's 4.2%), but swaps still price ~60% odds of a **September hike** under Chair Kevin Warsh. Rate-sensitive and long-duration assets carry policy risk.

- **Real economy of AI:** power is the bottleneck — GE Vernova's Q2 orders hit a record $24.2B (+88%), Duke announced the largest regulated capex plan ever ($103B), and data centers are reshaping utility demand.
- **Midterms Nov 3, 2026:** history says elevated volatility into the vote, limited lasting policy impact; analysts expect tariff/deregulation policy vectors to persist either way.
- **Gold:** ~$4,166/oz — still up ~$688 y/y but ~25% below its January 29 peak of $5,597 as the war premium deflates.

## How to size these against the ETF core

These lists are satellites, not a replacement for the diversified core in `portfolio-recommendation.md` (v2). Suggested blends of the *total* portfolio:

| Profile | ETF core | Tier 3 (low-vol) | Tier 2 (medium) | Tier 1 (high-vol) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Safety-first (matches your stated goal)** | 85% | 8% | 5% | 2% |
| **Balanced builder** | 70% | 12% | 12% | 6% |
| **Aggressive (only if you accept 25%+ drawdowns)** | 55% | 15% | 18% | 12% |

Rules that matter more than the picks: max ~3–5% in any single stock; buy in tranches over weeks (records + a possible September hike + midterm-season volatility = bad time to lump-sum); rebalance semiannually; hold Tier 1 names a full 3 years or don't buy them — they *will* see a 30% drawdown somewhere in the window.
