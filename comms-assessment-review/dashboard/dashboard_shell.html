This dashboard reads your assessment workbook and shows, per goal, whether the information that outcome needs actually reaches the people who need it. Everything here was computed by the workbook itself — the dashboard only displays it.

The elements you type into the workbook

Goal

The measurable outcome that anchors everything. Classed core (affects the company's short/medium-term sustainability) or aspirational (important — growth, development — but not sustainability-critical).

Topic

One named subject people must hear about, decomposed from exactly one goal. The controlled vocabulary that makes “what should happen” comparable with “what does happen.”

Required flow (a need)

“This person must hear this topic from this source, this fast.” Derived from the goal, signed off by the goal's owner before interviews.

Actual flow (an observation)

What interviews found really happens — recorded with evidence, from both ends. When the two ends contradict each other, the flow is disputed and quarantined from every score.

Carrier

Where an actual flow physically travels: a meeting or a channel, push (delivered) or pull (you must remember to check). A flow with no carrier is hearsay.

Meeting & Channel

The two kinds of venue. Meetings are audited on six evidence-anchored criteria, priced in person-minutes per week, and judged on whether the goals they claim match the goals they actually carry. Channels bring the properties meetings can't: asynchrony and durable records.

Decision

A recurring decision with one accountable owner on paper — compared against the room where it actually gets decided.

The verdicts the workbook computes

Flow status

Checked in strict order — missing → wrong producer → latency gap → channel mismatch → covered. The first failed check names the defect and its fix. Priority = criticality × severity, max 15.

Alignment

Theater = claims a goal, carries nothing of it. Unclaimed = carries a goal it never claims — real dependency, missing paperwork. The claim and the carry are measured independently on purpose: their disagreement is the signal.

Meeting class

VA (value-adding) · necessary NVA (feeds no goal but externally required) · NVA (cost with no cargo — consolidation candidate; cut only as an experiment).

Decision defect

a_absent — the owner isn't in the room where it's decided · none_observed — it drifts, decided nowhere · no_accountable — nobody owns it.

SPOF

A critical delivered flow with a single path and no system-of-record backup — it survives only as long as one person shows up. The fix ships with it: publish to a durable channel.
