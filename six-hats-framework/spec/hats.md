# The Six Hats — Canonical Definitions

Reference definitions for each mode. The per-hat prompt files in
[`../prompts/hats/`](../prompts/hats/) operationalize these; this file is the
plain-language source of truth.

---

## 🔵 Blue — Process / Meta

**Mode:** thinking about the thinking.

Blue manages the session rather than the topic. It sets the agenda, decides
which hat comes next, keeps the other hats honest (a Black comment during a
Yellow pass is a "hat violation"), and produces the summary. The facilitator
usually wears it, and it bookends every session.

- **Opening Blue** frames the problem, names the goal of the session, and (in
  `auto` mode) classifies the task modality.
- **Closing Blue** is the only pass allowed to read all the others. It
  synthesizes them into a decision/answer/plan, surfaces the sharpest tensions
  between hats, and lists next actions.

**Discipline:** stay meta. Blue does not add facts, opinions, or ideas of its
own during the run — it orchestrates and, at the end, synthesizes.

---

## ⚪ White — Facts & Information

**Mode:** neutral data.

What do we know? What don't we know? What would we need to find out, and how?
White separates hard fact from belief and hearsay, and is explicit about
confidence and provenance.

- Distinguish: verified fact / probable / claimed-but-unverified / unknown.
- Name the **gaps** as clearly as the knowns — missing data is White's output
  too.
- No interpretation, no recommendation. Just the information landscape.

**Discipline:** objective and neutral. If you catch yourself arguing a
position, you've switched hats. White's output is the *only* pass shared with
the other hats, so it must be clean.

---

## 🔴 Red — Emotion & Intuition

**Mode:** gut feeling.

How do you feel about this, right now, without justifying it? Hunches,
suspicions, aesthetic reactions, "something's off here," enthusiasm, dread.

The value is counter-intuitive: by giving feelings a legitimate, explicit
channel, they stop leaking into the hats that are supposed to be logical. An
unspoken bad feeling contaminates a Black-hat analysis; a spoken one is just
data.

**Discipline:** **no reasons.** Red is the one hat that must not justify itself.
State the feeling and its intensity, and move on. Do not let it turn into a
Black-hat critique wearing a Red hat.

---

## ⚫ Black — Caution & Critical Judgment

**Mode:** why this might fail.

Risks, flaws, dangers, weak points, downside scenarios, ways the plan meets
reality and loses. De Bono called it the most useful hat and the most overused —
critique is our default, which is exactly why the framework quarantines it to
its own pass so it can't smother the others.

**Discipline:** logical negativity. Every concern must be backed by a reason —
this is critique, not pessimism or mood. "I don't like it" is a Red statement;
"this fails if traffic spikes because there's no backpressure" is a Black one.
Black points at real, articulable failure modes.

---

## 🟡 Yellow — Optimism & Value

**Mode:** why this might work.

Benefits, upside, best-realistic case, what goes *right*, the value on offer and
who captures it. The constructive counterweight to Black.

For most people and models this is the *harder* hat, because threat-detection
is the default and genuine value-detection takes deliberate effort. That's the
whole reason it gets its own protected pass.

**Discipline:** logical positivity. Every benefit must be backed by a reason —
this is reasoned optimism, not Red-hat cheerfulness. "This feels great" is Red;
"this cuts onboarding from days to minutes because the config is inferred" is
Yellow.

---

## 🟢 Green — Creativity

**Mode:** new ideas and alternatives.

Options, variations, provocations, "what if," ways around the constraints,
things nobody has proposed yet. Draws on de Bono's older concept of *lateral
thinking* — deliberately moving sideways instead of digging the current hole
deeper.

- Generate alternatives, not just improvements to the one option on the table.
- Provocations are allowed to be wrong on purpose (de Bono's "po") if they
  shake loose a better idea.

**Discipline:** judgment is suspended. Green produces; it does not evaluate.
Killing an idea because it's risky is a Black move — save it for Black's pass.
Quantity and range over polish.

---

## The two asymmetries to remember

1. **Yellow is harder than Black.** Protect the Yellow pass; don't let it be a
   token paragraph.
2. **Red is unjustified by design.** It's the only hat where "because I said so"
   is the correct output. Everything else must carry reasons.
