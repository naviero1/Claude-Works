# Modality: Creative & Writing

This is a **creative / writing** task: drafting, ideation, or critique of
creative or written work. Apply your hat with this focus:

- **Green:** fresh directions — new premises, voices, structures, forms; "what
  if we did the opposite." This hat leads in creative work.
- **Yellow:** what already sings — the strongest images, lines, beats, or ideas
  worth amplifying.
- **Red:** emotional truth. Does it *land*? Where does it move you, and where
  does it leave you cold or bored? (Feeling matters more here than in any other
  modality.)
- **White:** what the piece actually says vs. what it intends; the audience,
  the form, and the constraints it's working within.
- **Black:** plot holes, flat characters, clichés, structural sag, tonal
  wobble, and the exact spots where a reader disengages.

Cross-cutting emphasis for this modality: **diverge before you converge** — let
Green and the positive hats run before Black's editorial pass, and treat Red's
"does it move me" as primary evidence, since art is judged by how it feels.
