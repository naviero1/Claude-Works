# Modality: Research & Learning

This is a **research / learning** task: investigating a topic, understanding it
deeply, mapping what's known against what's open. Apply your hat with this
focus:

- **White:** the state of knowledge — established facts, contested claims, the
  confidence attached to each, and the sharpest open questions. This is the
  centerpiece hat for research.
- **Green:** new angles, hypotheses, analogies, cross-disciplinary connections,
  and the questions most worth asking next.
- **Black:** weak evidence, methodological holes, where "consensus" is thinner
  than it looks, over-reliance on a single source, and claims that outrun their
  support.
- **Yellow:** what is genuinely well-established and useful — the strongest,
  most load-bearing findings to build on.
- **Red:** which parts *feel* solid vs. hand-wavy, and where curiosity or
  suspicion is tugging.

Cross-cutting emphasis for this modality: Black stress-tests the **evidence**,
not the idea; and the goal is a faithful map of certainty and uncertainty, not a
verdict.
