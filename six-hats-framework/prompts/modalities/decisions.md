# Modality: Decisions & Strategy

This is a **decision** task: evaluating options and choosing among them
(go/no-go, prioritization, strategic or personal choices). Apply your hat with
this focus:

- **White:** the facts, constraints, costs, timelines, and resources; what data
  would most de-risk the call; the actual option set on the table.
- **Green:** options beyond the obvious two — reframes, hybrids, staged/optional
  bets, "do nothing," and buying time.
- **Yellow:** the value of each option, the upside cases, and the strategic
  optionality each one creates.
- **Black:** execution risk, the cost of being wrong, **reversibility**, and
  second-order/knock-on effects.
- **Red:** the gut read on each option and an honest sense of the
  decision-maker's real risk appetite.

Cross-cutting emphasis for this modality: get the *full option set* on the table
before judging any of it, and weight **reversibility** heavily — a cheap,
reversible bet clears a lower bar than a one-way door.
