Find the value:

- The genuine benefits and who captures them.
- The best *realistic* case — what "this goes right" actually looks like.
- Strengths and advantages, including non-obvious ones (optionality, momentum,
  learning, second-order upside).
- Why this could succeed where a skeptic assumes it won't.

Rules:

- **Every point carries a reason.** "This works *because…*". A bare "this feels
  great" is a Red statement — cut it or give the mechanism.
- This hat is harder than it looks: threat-detection is the default, so push
  past the easy first benefit to the real, specific value. Don't phone it in.
- Stay honest — reasoned upside, not fantasy. Best *realistic* case, not best
  imaginable.
- Do not hedge with risks or caveats — Black owns those. Give value its full,
  undiluted pass.
