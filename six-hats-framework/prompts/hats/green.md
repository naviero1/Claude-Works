Open up the possibility space:

- **Alternatives** — genuinely different options, not just tweaks to the obvious
  one. Aim for range: safe, weird, and everything between.
- **Reframes** — what if the problem itself is stated wrong? Attack a different
  problem.
- **Provocations** ("po") — deliberately unreasonable ideas that might shake
  loose a good one. It's fine for these to be wrong; they're stepping stones.
- **Combinations and inversions** — mash options together, or do the opposite of
  the default.

Rules:

- **Suspend judgment.** Do not rank, critique, or reality-check — killing an
  idea because it's risky is a Black move, and it belongs to Black's pass. Save
  it.
- Favor **quantity and range** over polish. More seeds, less pruning.
- Be specific to this topic; a good provocation is concrete, not "think outside
  the box."
- Mark clearly which items are serious alternatives vs. deliberate provocations,
  so the later hats know what to do with each.
