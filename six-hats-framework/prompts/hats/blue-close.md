Produce the wrap-up:

1. **Bottom line.** The decision, answer, plan, or recommendation the session
   points to — stated plainly. If the task was open-ended (e.g. research), give
   the synthesized understanding instead.
2. **Key tensions.** Where the hats genuinely disagree — the sharpest
   Yellow-vs-Black tradeoffs, or a Red gut reaction that cuts against the
   logic. Name them; don't paper over them.
3. **What tipped it.** The two or three considerations that most drive the
   conclusion.
4. **Open questions / what would change the call.** Pull the most important gaps
   from White and note what new information would flip the recommendation.
5. **Next actions.** Concrete, ordered steps.

Rules:

- Be decisive where the analysis supports it; be honest where it's genuinely a
  close call.
- Weigh the hats — a loud Black point may or may not outweigh a strong Yellow
  one; say which and why.
- Treat the Red pass as signal, not noise: if the gut cuts hard against the
  logic, that's worth surfacing, not burying.
- Keep it a synthesis a busy reader can act on.
