## Tier 1 — the billion-user defaults
- ChatGPT: 900M weekly actives (OpenAI official, TechCrunch Feb 27 2026); crossed 1B
  MONTHLY on mobile+web Jun 2026 (Reuters/Sensor Tower); 53.9% of world chatbot web
  visits ~5.6B/mo (Similarweb May 2026 — down from 86.7% a year earlier); 44% of US
  adults have used it (Pew); 82% of AI-using developers (SO 2025). WHY: first mover,
  brand-as-verb, best free all-rounder. Still only ~10% of the global population
  weekly (a16z) — not saturated.
- Gemini: 400M MAU (I/O May 2025) → 900M+ MAU (I/O May 2026); 27.9% of chatbot
  visits, +~450% YoY (Similarweb May 2026); 24% of US adults (Pew). AI Overviews
  reach 2.5B/mo (Pichai, CNBC May 19 2026). WHY: bundling — Android, Gmail, Docs,
  Search.
- Copilot: 17% of US adults (Pew), 14% weekly (Edison) — #3 with people; but 1.3% of
  chatbot destination visits (Similarweb) and #23 a16z mobile. WHY: it's IN Word/
  Outlook/Teams/Windows and IT already approved Microsoft. The cleanest usage ≠
  preference story in the data.
- Meta AI: 1B+ monthly (Zuckerberg, May 2025 — last hard number); +60% daily
  interactions after the Jul 2026 rebuild (Q2 call). Lives inside WhatsApp/Instagram
  — barely exists as a chosen app (#21 a16z mobile).

## Tier 2 — chosen for the job (fastest growing)
- Claude: 9.2% of chatbot visits ~952M/mo, +855% YoY — fastest growth of the majors
  (Similarweb May 2026); #3 site overall (Aug 2026); paid subs +200% YoY (a16z);
  6% of US adults (Pew) — a prosumer tool picked on quality (writing, coding).
- Perplexity: #8 web a16z; 1.3% of visits — famous-but-niche; persists on cited,
  sourced answers.
- Gemini Notebook (ex-NotebookLM): #30 web a16z; the your-documents-only tool
  (rename Jul 16 2026 — r12).
- DeepSeek: 4.1% share; #4 web a16z; with ChatGPT+Gemini = ~90% of time spent in
  AI-assistant apps (Sensor Tower Q1 2026); heavy Asia skew.

## Tier 3 — AI came to tools people already had (a16z 6th-ed. methodology change)

## Frequency & behavior framing
- Work (US, Gallup May 2026): 52% of employees use AI at least a few times a year
  (first majority), 30% weekly+, 15% daily. Jobs: writing/editing 51%, search/
  research 49%, problem-solving 39%.
- Consumers: 49% of US adults use chatbots, ~25% daily; 60% read AI search summaries
  (Pew Feb 2026). 65% of Americans weekly by May 2026, +36M in three months
  (Edison/SSRS).
- 86% of workplace AI users treat output as a STARTING POINT, not a final product
  (Microsoft WTI 2026) — the trust-but-verify stat.
- Dev slice (SO 2025): 84% use/plan AI tools; 51% of professionals daily; trust low
  (~29–33% trust accuracy; top gripe "almost right," 66%).

## Reddit signal — ANECDOTAL (secondary roundups; Reddit blocks crawlers)
Consistent across four 2026 roundups (Tom's Guide; beginnersinai.org Aug 21 2026;
izoate.com Aug 23 2026; aitooldiscovery.com Sep 1 2026):
- #1 behavioral finding: people run a small STACK, not one tool (ChatGPT daily
  driver + Claude for serious writing + Perplexity for sourced answers).
- Per-job consensus (paraphrased, no verifiable thread links): writing→Claude ·
  search→Perplexity · your-documents→NotebookLM · meetings→Otter/Fireflies ·
  images→Midjourney (artistic)/DALL-E-in-ChatGPT (convenient) · coding→Claude
  Code/Cursor.
- Recurring WHYs: integration beats standalone; free tiers decide first contact;
  minimal-editing output wins retention; "still using after 3 months" beats hype.
- One peer-reviewed Reddit datum: heavy emotional-support/companionship use
  (PubMed 41037971, 2025) — matches companion-app dwell times (Character.AI 15:58
  avg visit vs ChatGPT 6:40, Similarweb Aug 2026).
