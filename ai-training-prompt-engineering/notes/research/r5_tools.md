# R5 — The AI Tool Landscape (verified as of August 21, 2026)

Research notes for the "Prompt Engineering, Generative AI, and Agentic AI" corporate training (medical-device supplier/quality engineers + business professionals). All claims verified via web search/fetch on 2026-08-21 unless marked UNVERIFIED. **Teaching frame: rankings shift monthly — teach each tool's forte, not "X is the best."**

## Part A — Chat assistants and their current models (Aug 2026)

### 1. ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- **Current models:** The GPT-5.6 family — three tiers named **Sol** (flagship), **Terra** (enterprise/mid), and **Luna** (high-volume/free tier) — reached general availability July 9, 2026 (source: https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6-preferred-model-microsoft-365-copilot/ ; https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/gpt-5-6-microsoft-365-copilot-team-playbook-2026). On Aug 6, 2026 OpenAI folded the separate "Instant" and "reasoning" models into a single **GPT-5.6 Sol** experience for Plus/Pro users, with a slider controlling how much "thought" goes into a response; **GPT-5.6 Luna** became the default for Free/Go users with unlimited text chat and a "Think" button (source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes ; https://releasebot.io/updates/openai).
- **On the horizon (not shipped):** OpenAI introduced **Astra** on Aug 1, 2026 via ten solved open problems in mathematics/theoretical CS — no release date, pricing, or ChatGPT availability announced; unclear whether it ships as GPT-6 (source: https://felloai.com/all-we-know-about-chatgpt-6/). Treat Astra as a research preview, not a product.
- **ChatGPT now has three modes:** Chat (conversation), **Work** (agentic, see Part B), and **Codex** (software development) (source: https://aitoolsreview.co.uk/insights/chatgpt-work).
- **Context window:** GPT-5.6 tiers support ~1.05M tokens (source: https://www.techcompare.app/token-counter/token-limits-by-model — secondary; OpenAI's own spec page not fetched, treat exact number as approximate).
- **Forte (one-liner):** The broadest all-rounder with the largest user base — strong general reasoning, integrated image generation (ChatGPT Images 2.0 is a leading photorealistic image generator — source: https://kubeez.com/blog/best-ai-models-image-video-sound-2026), voice, and the new Work agent mode bundled into paid plans.
- **Caveat:** Rapid renaming/model churn (Instant vs. reasoning vs. Sol/Luna, deprecated "agent mode") confuses users; enterprise data-governance settings matter before pasting regulated content.

### 2. Claude (Anthropic)
- **Current lineup (4 tiers), all verified on vendor docs/news:**
  - **Claude Fable 5** — released June 9, 2026; Anthropic's most capable widely released model, "Mythos-class," built for demanding reasoning and long-horizon agentic work; 1M-token context, up to 128K output; $10/$50 per M tokens; always-on adaptive thinking; includes safety classifiers that can refuse high-risk requests (cyber/bio) and fall back to another Claude model (source: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/introducing-claude-fable-5-and-claude-mythos-5 ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/09/anthropic-mythos-claude-fable-5.html).
  - **Claude Opus 5** — released July 24, 2026; positioned as the cost-efficient near-peer of Fable 5 ($5/$25 per M tokens), aimed at complex agentic coding (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-claude-opus-5-ai-fable-5-cost.html ; https://gizmodo.com/anthropic-releases-new-claude-model-positions-it-as-a-cost-efficient-version-of-fable-5-2000790486).
  - **Claude Sonnet 5** — launched June 30, 2026; the balanced default across Anthropic consumer/developer products ($2/$10) (source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5).
  - **Claude Haiku 4.5** — fast/cheap tier ($1/$5), 200K context (source: https://www.secondtalent.com/resources/every-claude-ai-model-explained-compared/ — secondary roundup consistent with Anthropic pricing pages).
  - **Claude Mythos 5** — same capabilities as Fable 5 *without* safety classifiers; limited release to approved customers via "Project Glasswing" only — not a general product (source: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/introducing-claude-fable-5-and-claude-mythos-5).
- **Notable event (good governance teaching moment):** On June 12, 2026 the US government applied export controls to Fable 5/Mythos 5 after Amazon researchers found a safeguard-bypass enabling vulnerability discovery; Anthropic suspended access globally, and Fable 5 was redeployed worldwide July 1, 2026 after controls were lifted June 30 (source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5).
- **Forte:** Leader in agentic/coding work and long documents (Sonnet 5/Opus 5/Fable 5 all 1M context); #1 on LMArena text (Fable 5, ~1525 Elo, Aug 2026) and top of SWE-bench Verified (see Part C); strong "careful professional writing" reputation.
- **Caveat:** Fable 5 can refuse in classified high-risk domains (by design) and costs premium rates; no free-tier access to top models.

### 3. Google Gemini
- **Current models:** **Gemini 3** launched March 2026 as Google's flagship ("most intelligent model"), with **Gemini 3 Deep Think** mode for hard math/logic (source: https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-3/). On July 21, 2026 Google shipped **Gemini 3.6 Flash** (new "workhorse," ~17% fewer tokens), **3.5 Flash-Lite**, and **3.5 Flash Cyber** — notably no "3.5 Pro" (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/21/google-releases-three-new-gemini-models-but-no-3-5-pro/). Gemini 3.1 Pro remains the Pro-tier model referenced across integrations (source: https://yingtu.ai/en/blog/gemini-3-1-model-lineup — secondary).
- **Multimodal stack:** **Nano Banana 2** (Gemini 3.1 Flash Image) is the default image model in the Gemini app since Feb 2026 (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/26/google-launches-nano-banana-2-model-with-faster-image-generation/); **Veo 3.1** for video; **Deep Think** ships via the AI Ultra subscription (source: https://deepmind.google/models/ ; https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-google-ai-models-explained/).

- **Forte:** Deepest multimodal stack (image/video/voice) + Workspace integration (Gmail/Docs/Sheets) + huge context for giant document sets.
- **Caveat:** Model naming sprawl (3, 3.1, 3.5, 3.6 across Pro/Flash/Lite tiers) is genuinely confusing; the best reasoning (Deep Think) sits behind the Ultra tier.

### 4. Microsoft Copilot (M365)

Note: Microsoft relabels models as "Quick Response" / "Think Deeper" rather than Sol/Terra/Luna (source: https://office-watch.com/2026/gpt-5-6-microsoft-365-copilot/).

### 5. Perplexity
- **What it is:** Search-first assistant that answers with inline **citations**; Pro Search reads 300+ sources per query (source: https://www.index.dev/blog/perplexity-ai-features-statistics).
- **Model Council** (Max/Enterprise Max only): runs one query across several frontier models simultaneously and a synthesizer model merges them, showing where models agree/disagree. Current default set as of Aug 2026: **GPT-5.6 Sol (Thinking), Claude Opus 5 (Thinking), Gemini 3.1 Pro (Thinking)** (source: https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/13641704-what-is-model-council ; https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/introducing-model-council).
- **Comet browser:** Perplexity's AI-native browser — desktop since July 2025, worldwide free since Oct 2025, Android Nov 2025, iOS March 18, 2026 (hit #3 on US App Store in 48h); embeds the assistant in every tab with cross-tab summarization (source: https://beginnersinai.org/whats-new-perplexity-2026/ ; https://www.switchtools.io/blog/perplexity-comet-ai-agent-browser). Pricing Aug 2026: Pro $20/mo, Max $200/mo, Enterprise from $40/seat (source: https://suprmind.ai/hub/perplexity/pricing/).
- **Forte:** Research questions where you need *sourced, checkable* answers fast — the citation habit is built in.
- **Caveat:** Citations point to sources; they don't guarantee the source is right or that the summary is faithful — click through before quoting in a regulated document.

### 6. Grok (xAI → SpaceX)
- **Corporate note (verified):** SpaceX acquired xAI on Feb 2, 2026 in an all-stock deal valuing the combination at $1.25T (largest private merger in history; X included, Tesla not); rebranded under a SpaceX AI unit, **"SpaceXAI"** (May 2026); IPO June 12, 2026 at $1.77T (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/11/musk-announces-xai-re-org-following-key-departures-spacex-merger.html ; https://techjournal.org/spacex-xai-merger).
- **Current model:** **Grok 4.6**, released Aug 12, 2026 — same 1.5T-parameter "V9" foundation as Grok 4.5 (July 8, 2026) with improved post-training; 500K context; $2/$6 per M tokens (<200K prompt); scored 61 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (source: https://emergent.sh/news/grok-46-officially-launched ; https://www.fullstack.com/labs/resources/blog/grok-4-5-a-closer-look-at-xais-latest-model). Grok 4.5 was trained in partnership with Cursor for long-running multi-repo agent jobs (source: https://www.fullstack.com/labs/resources/blog/grok-4-5-a-closer-look-at-xais-latest-model). Grok 4.7 (new 2.1T foundation) expected late Aug/early Sept 2026 (source: https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/grok-4-6-preview).
- **Forte:** Aggressive price/performance and speed; real-time X/social data; strong coding-agent focus.
- **Caveat:** Fewer enterprise-governance guardrails historically; the model family's positioning and pricing change very fast — check current terms before standardizing on it.

## Part B — Agentic tools

**Teaching definition:** a chat assistant *answers*; an agent *does* — it plans multi-step work, uses tools (files, browsers, apps), checks its own results, and iterates. Analogy: chat AI is a knowledgeable colleague on the phone; agentic AI is a contractor you hand keys to. (Defensible: matches vendor definitions of agentic loops.) Every agent adds real risk: it acts with *your* permissions.

### Claude Code (Anthropic)
- **What it is:** Anthropic's agentic coding *and general automation* agent. Surfaces: terminal/CLI, desktop app (macOS/Linux/Windows), web (claude.ai/code), VS Code/JetBrains extensions, mobile, Slack, GitHub Actions (source: https://claude.com/claude-code). It works on a real folder: opens PDFs, reads spreadsheets, renames files, writes briefs, saves results back as ordinary files — which is why non-developers increasingly use it for file/data work (source: https://ccforeveryone.com/guides/claude-code-for-non-developers ; https://medium.com/@vinayanand2/beyond-the-chatbox-a-non-technical-guide-to-mastering-claude-code-in-2026-8f7acd3a6e7d).
- **Skills:** reusable instruction packages (folders with a SKILL.md) that make workflows repeatable; large community ecosystem (official + community skill repos with 150K–240K+ GitHub stars across frameworks) (source: https://www.firecrawl.dev/blog/best-claude-code-skills ; https://www.totalum.app/blog/claude-code-skills-totalum). Also supports subagents, hooks, scheduled Routines, and MCP integrations (source: https://claude.com/claude-code).
- **Who should reach for it:** developers first; also analysts/quality engineers comfortable pointing an agent at a folder of files (e.g., "extract every CAPA reference from these 40 PDFs into a spreadsheet").
- **NOT for:** people who want zero setup and a pure chat window; unreviewed changes to controlled documents.
- **Risk notes:** it executes commands and edits files — use permission modes, keep it in a sandboxed folder, review diffs like you'd review a junior engineer's work.

### Claude Cowork (Anthropic)
- **What it is:** Claude Code's sibling for *general knowledge work* — an autonomous agent that organizes files, creates documents (Excel/PowerPoint/reports), and synthesizes research: "describe the outcome, step away, come back to finished work." Launched as a desktop research preview Jan 12, 2026; expanded to web + iOS/Android (beta, Max subscribers) July 7, 2026; connectors for Google Drive, Gmail, DocuSign, FactSet etc. (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/07/the-coding-agent-wars-are-spilling-into-the-rest-of-the-office-claude-cowork/ ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/24/anthropic-claude-cowork-office-worker.html ; https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork). As of Aug 12, 2026 the Claude-in-Chrome side panel runs full Cowork sessions with skills/connectors (source: https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/12/claude-cowork-chrome/).
- **Best at:** multi-file office work — folder cleanup, document assembly, spreadsheet building, research-to-deliverable.
- **NOT for:** real-time collaboration inside Office apps (that's Copilot's turf); tasks needing enterprise tenant governance your IT hasn't approved.
- **Risk:** same as any file-touching agent — scope its folder access; verify outputs before they enter the QMS.

### ChatGPT agent mode → **ChatGPT Work** (OpenAI)
- The older "ChatGPT agent" was retired; OpenAI's help center now directs users to **ChatGPT Work** (launched July 9, 2026) — an agentic mode beside Chat and Codex that connects to Slack, Gmail, Drive, Salesforce etc. via a plugins directory, runs scheduled tasks, and works for hours to produce finished spreadsheets, decks, reports, or small web apps; bundled into Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise, powered by GPT-5.6 (source: https://aitoolsreview.co.uk/insights/chatgpt-work ; https://www.usecarly.com/blog/chatgpt-agent-mode/ — deprecation notice appeared early Aug 2026).
- **Best at:** deliverable-producing office tasks for people already living in ChatGPT.
- **NOT for:** work requiring your company's tenant controls; browser-heavy flows are pushed to OpenAI's cloud browser instead.
- **Risk:** connected-account permissions (email, CRM) are the attack surface — grant minimum scopes.

### Microsoft Copilot agents
- **Researcher:** multi-step research agent over web + your work content; supports OpenAI *and* Claude models, and a "Critique" flow where a GPT-drafted report gets a second reasoning pass from Claude (source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/use-model-choice-in-the-researcher-agent).
- **Analyst:** data-analysis reasoning agent (chain-of-thought over spreadsheets/data) — part of the same M365 agent family (source: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2025/09/24/expanding-model-choice-in-microsoft-365-copilot/).
- **Excel Agent Mode:** users can select Claude as the model for data-analysis tasks in Agent Mode in Excel; Word/Excel/PowerPoint agents can be powered by Claude (source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/use-claude-with-researcher-in-microsoft-365-copilot ; https://www.directionsonmicrosoft.com/reports/m365-copilot-adds-choice-and-risk-with-anthropics-claude/).
- **Copilot Studio:** low-code builder for custom enterprise agents; Anthropic joined the multi-model lineup (Claude Sonnet/Opus options) (source: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/blog/copilot-studio/anthropic-joins-the-multi-model-lineup-in-microsoft-copilot-studio/).
- **Best at:** governed, tenant-controlled agents inside the Microsoft stack — usually the path of least resistance for a regulated company.
- **NOT for:** bleeding-edge capability the day it ships (features roll out by tenant/region).
- **Risk:** agents inherit your M365 permissions; "Directions on Microsoft" explicitly frames the Anthropic option as "choice *and risk*" — data flows to a Microsoft subprocessor (Anthropic) that admins must consciously allow (source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/connect-to-ai-subprocessor).

### OpenClaw (open-source personal agent) — the cautionary tale that's also a phenomenon
- **What it is:** free, open-source autonomous agent that runs **locally** on your machine, is controlled through **messaging apps** (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Discord), plugs into LLMs (Claude, GPT, DeepSeek...), keeps persistent local memory, and uses a SKILL.md-based skills system (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenClaw).
- **History (verified):** created by Austrian engineer **Peter Steinberger**; released as "Warelay" Nov 24, 2025 → "CLAWDIS" Dec 3, 2025 → **Clawdbot** Jan 2, 2026 → **Moltbot** Jan 27, 2026 (after Anthropic trademark complaints) → **OpenClaw** Jan 30, 2026 (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenClaw). Steinberger joined OpenAI Feb 14–15, 2026 (working on Codex/agents); a non-profit **OpenClaw Foundation** now stewards the project (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/openclaw-creator-peter-steinberger-joins-openai/ ; https://steipete.me/posts/2026/openclaw).
- **Scale:** ~247,000 GitHub stars / 47,700 forks as of March 2, 2026; roughly half a million systems running it worldwide (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenClaw ; https://www.fastcompany.com/91550800/how-peter-steinberger-built-openclaw).
- **The maintainer's own warning (use this quote in training):** a maintainer known as Shadow: *"If you can't understand how to run a command line, this is far too dangerous of a project for you to use safely"* (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenClaw ; https://conscia.com/blog/the-openclaw-security-crisis/).
- **Security incidents (verified):** CVE-2026-25253, a 1-click RCE (CVSS 8.8), patched in v2026.1.29 on Jan 30, 2026, disclosed alongside two command-injection advisories (source: https://ccb.belgium.be/advisories/warning-critical-vulnerability-openclaw-allows-1-click-remote-code-execution-when); by Feb 9, 2026 SecurityScorecard found 135,000+ exposed instances across 82 countries, ~12,800 exploitable (source: https://primerogueinc.com/blog/openclaw-security-crisis-structurally-broken-in-february-2026-what-naive-deployers-need-to-know-before-its-too-late/); Cisco found a third-party skill doing data exfiltration/prompt injection — the skills marketplace lacks vetting (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenClaw); China's MIIT issued a high-risk warning Feb 5, 2026 and by March 2026 barred state enterprises from running it (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenClaw). Also CVE-2026-32922 (privilege escalation) reported later in 2026 (source: https://www.armosec.io/blog/cve-2026-32922-openclaw-privilege-escalation-cloud-security/). The "MoltMatch" incident (Feb 2026): an agent autonomously created a dating profile, using a real person's photos without consent (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenClaw).
- **Status Aug 2026:** active, Foundation-stewarded, still the flagship DIY personal agent.
- **Best at:** hobbyist/power-user personal automation ("my agent texts me my calendar briefing").
- **NOT for:** corporate use at a regulated company. Full stop, for now — no vendor accountability, unvetted skills, direct message-app control of a machine with your credentials.
- **Teaching value:** perfect illustration of why "agent + broad permissions + untrusted content = prompt-injection risk."

### Coding IDEs
- **Cursor:** AI-first IDE; Composer mode for multi-file refactoring, cloud "background agents" you review later; crossed $1B ARR; ~72% completion-acceptance in one 2026 comparison (source: https://daily.dev/blog/ai-code-editor-comparison-cursor-vs-vs-code-vs-windsurf/ ; https://codeant.ai/blogs/best-ai-code-editor-cursor-vs-windsurf-vs-copilot). Best for professional developers on complex projects; not a business-user tool.
- **Windsurf → Devin Desktop:** Cognition (maker of Devin) acquired Windsurf July 14, 2025; renamed **Devin Desktop** June 2, 2026. Devin is now one brand across Devin Desktop (IDE), Devin Cloud (autonomous agent), Devin CLI, and Devin Review; supports the open Agent Client Protocol so Codex/Claude/OpenCode agents run inside the same editor; combined company grew to $500M+ run rate (source: https://devin.ai/blog/windsurf-2-0 ; https://news.creeta.com/en/windsurf-renamed-devin-desktop-2026/). Best for teams wanting an autonomous "junior engineer" model (assign issue → get PR); risk: autonomous PRs still need human review — Devin's early-2024 hype-vs-reality gap is a standard cautionary example.
- **GitHub Copilot:** still ~42% market share, $10/mo; agent can be assigned a GitHub issue, write code, open a PR, and respond to review feedback (source: https://daily.dev/blog/ai-code-editor-comparison-cursor-vs-vs-code-vs-windsurf/ ; https://devstarsj.github.io/2026/04/04/ai-coding-assistants-copilot-cursor-windsurf-comparison-2026/). Best "default" for enterprises already on GitHub; least aggressive agent autonomy of the three.

### Browser agents
- **Perplexity Comet:** AI-native browser (see Part A) — assistant in every tab, agentic actions on pages; free tier exists. Risk: an agent that reads every page you browse is an exfiltration channel if compromised — early Comet prompt-injection demos (2025) made this concrete.
- **Claude in Chrome:** extension letting Claude see the page and act (click, type, navigate, fill forms) using your logins; open beta on all paid Claude plans as of early Aug 2026; on Aug 12, 2026 the side panel became a full Cowork session (Max/Team, rolling to Pro) (source: https://claude.com/claude-in-chrome ; https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/12/claude-cowork-chrome/). Risk note: a July 2026 flaw could have let rogue *other* extensions piggyback on its Gmail access — patched, but instructive (source: https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2026/07/claude-for-chrome-flaw-could-let-rogue-extensions-access-your-gmail).

### Automation platforms with agent features
- **n8n:** open-source/self-hostable workflow automation; v2.0 (Jan 2026) added native LangChain integration, ~70 AI nodes, persistent agent memory, RAG/vector-DB nodes, human-in-the-loop patterns, and true agent loops (use tool → check result → iterate) (source: https://hatchworks.com/blog/ai-agents/n8n-vs-zapier/ ; https://nodesify.com/blog/n8n-workflow-automation-guide-2026). Best for technical teams wanting self-hosted, auditable automation (a plus for regulated data residency); NOT for non-technical users — it's a builder's tool.
- **Zapier:** 8,000+ app connectors; **Zapier Agents** (autonomous AI teammates), Copilot (natural-language Zap builder), and an MCP server exposing 30,000+ actions to external LLMs; agents are an add-on layer atop Zaps (source: https://startupowl.com/reviews/zapier ; https://medium.com/@automation.labs/zapier-vs-make-vs-n8n-in-2026-where-ai-agents-actually-fit-1edbbeff85f3). Best for business users wiring SaaS apps together without code; NOT for complex branching logic or self-hosting.
