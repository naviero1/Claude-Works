through a per-slide exception request. That is safe but costs a review round for
exactly the slides most likely to need it. Recommendation: a standing, recorded
exception — size increases are pre-approved where the wording is unchanged and
the current size is below 20pt; everything else still goes through you. If you
prefer strict per-slide exceptions, I will comply and simply batch the requests.

both ways in the pilot decision (formatting-only vs. prompts + small crop), or
build formatting-only and show the crop variant as a labeled alternate in the
