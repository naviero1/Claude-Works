## Visual direction

Please keep the existing professional character and make the composition more deliberate.

Suggested palette: warm off-white `#F7F8F6`, charcoal `#232A31`, teal `#0E7C7B`, muted amber `#C47F17`, and quiet gray `#A7B0B5`. These refine the established system; they are not a reason to recolor the entire course.

Use subtle background areas to support hierarchy. Reserve dark backgrounds mainly for dividers. Avoid patterns behind text, excessive cards, unrelated stock images, or decorative graphs.

Aim for approximately 22–24-point projected instructions where the layout supports it. Short labels and source captions may be smaller. Reflow the composition before shrinking the main text. Preserve essential qualifications rather than cutting them to meet a word quota.

A useful visual should explain the exercise output or a finding. Not every slide needs a chart. Screenshots should be cropped so the relevant detail is readable; retain editable source workbooks and charts.

Use the slide for the action and essential requirements. Keep complete prompts, detailed schemas, expected outputs, common mistakes, extensions, and keys available in the notes and reference materials.

Keep filenames participants need to open or save. Move repository paths, build history, and instructor logistics into notes.
