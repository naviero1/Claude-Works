# From Prompts to Agents: facilitation plan and audit

The live course is 60 minutes, including 30 minutes of protected application. The deck retains the 69 source slides, adds the requested requirements-quality slide, and includes a separate detailed appendix. Classification follows the learning contribution, not slide density. Appendix pages have no additional live allocation.

## Outcomes and sequence

Participants explain ASK versus DELEGATE, write a usable task brief, check a result against its source, define an approval boundary, and save a reusable prompt. The familiar requirements-writing analogy now precedes prompt construction. The new requirements-quality slide compares clear, complete, singular, feasible, and verifiable requirements with their prompting benefits. The prompt examples make role, context, tone, format, and checks explicit requirement types. Slides 91–96 provide an artifact-specific menu; the companion Requirements by Artifact catalog adds further types and acceptance evidence.

## Structural and content decisions

- Move the requirements analogy before prompt anatomy and add the requested requirements-quality lesson.
- Replace overloaded live slides with readable, focused explanations and explicit participant actions.
- Keep deep prompt anatomy and mission-brief detail in the appendix and the existing evidence maps.
- Keep ASK versus DELEGATE central; a long response or a clarifying question alone does not establish agentic work.
- Distinguish model self-review from source verification and prompt instructions from enforceable permissions.
- Use one manageable supplier calculation live, with an independently calculated fuller scorecard in the appendix.
- Supply the fictional email source and answer key. Keep the quotation comparison optional until its source quotations are supplied.
- Remove obsolete block references, contradictory exercise timers, blanket template requirements, and unqualified claims about current product capabilities.
- Keep the next requested exercise-and-builder redesign in the Claude handoff; this deck establishes the facilitation baseline first.
