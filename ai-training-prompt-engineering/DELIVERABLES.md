# Deliverables

The five artifacts of **From Prompts to Agents**. Everything in `deliverables/` is
participant-facing and self-contained; everything else in this package is support
material, indexed in `REFERENCES.md`. All names, companies, and numbers are
fictional and generated for practice.

| # | File | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `deliverables/From_Prompts_to_Agents_Facilitated_60_Minute.pptx` | **The presentation.** The facilitated course: 70 live slides — foundations plus the nine-step protected application block (about 72:50 of full content with an owner-approved 60-minute flex rule in the facilitation plan) — plus a 33-page reference appendix. Speaker notes on every slide carry MODE / TIME / PURPOSE / LAND THIS / SAY / DO / BRIDGE / IF LATE. |
| 2 | `deliverables/Course_Workbook.xlsx` | **The course workbook.** Open its INDEX tab first. The fictional supplier dataset (raw + checked) and the exercise tabs in training order — `1-Analyze-Data`, `Excel-Charts`, `2-Build-Dashboard`, `3-Present-Findings`, `4-Summarize-Email`, `Quote-Extract`, `Quote-Compare`, `Research`, with `5-Explain-Clearly` as the reference exercise — plus two instructor key tabs. |
| 3 | `deliverables/Prompt_Template_Creator.html` | **The prompt creator.** Task-first builder: pick one of the nine task families (training order), choose requirements (each with its acceptance check), and the prompt assembles — editable, offline, single file. The full element taxonomy sits behind "Advanced". |
| 4 | `deliverables/From_Prompts_to_Agents_Course.pdf` | **The course PDF.** The extended written edition of the course, cover to cover: foundations, prompt engineering as requirements writing, the nine-step worked sequence on one dataset, agentic delegation, reusable prompts, and the reference appendix. For self-study or review after the live session. |
| 5 | `deliverables/From_Prompts_to_Agents_Cheat_Sheet.pdf` | **The course cheat sheet.** One printable page: the requirement skeleton, the 14-type menu, the quality bar, a before/after example, acceptance checks, and ASK vs DELEGATE. |

## How they connect

The presentation teaches; the workbook is where the exercises are done; the
course PDF is the same course in book form; the cheat sheet is the one-page
distillation; the prompt creator turns the course's requirement menus into
reusable prompts. One fictional supplier dataset, uploaded once at intake, runs through the whole data arc; the email thread, the quotation documents, and the research pack extend the same case.

Support files, prepared fallbacks, instructor material, and editable sources are
indexed in `REFERENCES.md`.
