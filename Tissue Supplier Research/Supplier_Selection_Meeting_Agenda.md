## Agenda (~45–60 min)

**1. Purpose & desired outcomes — 5 min**
Frame the decision and what "done" looks like today.

**2. Confirm the requirements — 15 min**
- Weekly hog volume band (200–3,000/wk) and the exception for organ/tissue specialists
- Must slaughter hogs; USDA-inspected and/or APHIS-listed; humane handling & culture (the "Nahunta standard")
- Tissue blocks we must secure: **pelvic, bariatric, thoracic** + organ harvesting
- *Decision:* is anything a hard gate vs. a preference?

**3. Confirm the strategy / operating model — 10 min**
- **Model A** (we send harvesters + rent space) vs **Model B** (they harvest, we send inspectors/trainers) vs **buy direct**
- EU-export implication: do we need suppliers to be APHIS-listed for TRACES? (gates the field)
- *Decision:* preferred model, and whether it varies by region

**4. Agree decision criteria & weighting — 10 min**
- How we score: volume, certification, welfare/enforcement history, model fit, organ capability, cost
- *Decision:* the weighting we'll rank candidates against

**5. Review regions & shortlist — 10 min**
- NC (primary), then SC/TN/VA, then Iowa/Midwest; current front-runners
- *Decision:* which candidates advance to outreach

**6. Next steps & owners — 5 min**
- Who contacts whom, by when; how we compare quotes/volumes on return
