## Agenda (~60 min)

**1) Introduction — 5 min**
Preparation to onboard a new tissue supplier given lead time.

**2) Confirm Requirements (current) — 25 min**
- **Volume: OPEN — capacity is no longer a filter.** No plant is excluded for being too small
  or too large. Every supplier is listed and classified into a tier instead:
  **Small 0–300/wk · Medium 350–2,500/wk · Medium-High 2,500–5,000/wk · High >5,000/wk.**
  *Discuss:* which tier(s) do we actually want, and does tier change the operating model
  (Small → Model A / aggregation; Medium → single-plant program; Medium-High → regional/cull-sow plants; High → Model B or a biologics arm)?
- **Certifications — two paths, agreed in the 2026-08 meeting:** a slaughterhouse **we harvest at** needs only
  **USDA/FSIS inspection** (APHIS not required of the plant — we assemble the model at ATM and that carries the export);
  a **direct tissue supplier** who ships us finished blocks **must be able to hold APHIS**.
  *NC state inspection is accepted by APHIS as equivalent to federal — **only NC**, which is why Nahunta IS exportable.*
  Humane culture: not flagged for animal cruelty; documented humane-handling actions drop a plant to CAUTION.
- Other requirements other than geographic.
- **How do we want to weight these requirements?**

**3) Review Regions — 10 min**
Review regions and weight in geographical advantages.
- **Model A prefers a 120-mile radius, all slaughterhouses.** Distances in the roster are real road miles
  from the Pikeville NC anchor. *Confirm the anchor* — if it should be ATM or another base, the column is recomputable.
- The four eastern-NC candidates all sit inside the radius, so the choice between them is not distance and not
  volume — it is the specific constraint on each.

**4) Confirm Strategy & Operating Model — 15 min**
- **Model A** — we harvest with our technicians (rent space, or by piece?).
- **Model B** — they harvest, we instruct.

**5) Next Steps — 5 min**
What is our MO to approach each of these. *Parking lot: revisit the questionnaire.*
