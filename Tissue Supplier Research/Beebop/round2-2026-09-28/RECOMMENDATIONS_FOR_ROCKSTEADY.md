- The ranking now puts evidence of fit ahead of novelty or the number of unknowns a call could resolve. Missing volume data earn no positive credit. This is an ordinal working recommendation, not a quantified probability of success.

## New evidence since the screenshot

Rocksteady’s commit 1cb841f introduced state-level hog totals. The National Agricultural Statistics Service’s [Livestock Slaughter 2025 Summary, table on page 56](https://esmis.nal.usda.gov/sites/default/release-files/795870/lsan0426.txt) confirms 7,841 federally inspected hogs across 16 Virginia plants and 14,070 across 8 South Carolina plants in 2025. These are annual head counts.

This evidence weakens EcoFriendly’s volume-replacement case. It is retained at #4 as an early harvesting-service qualification call, not because category three proves substantial pig volume. A state total is not this plant’s count. A historical average is not a current weekly capacity limit, and the data do not establish how many animals meet the live-weight requirement.

Sources distinguish the [aggregate category definition](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/24/2026-05746/revising-establishment-size-definitions) from [EcoFriendly’s own service description](https://www.ecofriendly.com/services). All four swine classes, certification claims or a custom processing menu do not establish organ rights, available room, trained collection staff or willingness to negotiate.
