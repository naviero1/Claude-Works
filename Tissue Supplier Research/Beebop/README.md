## Scope of this snapshot

- Mandatory geography: within 120 miles of Durham, North Carolina. The 120-mile radius governs eligibility. Road distance measures logistics and is not a separate exclusion.
- Planning origin: Durham City Hall, 101 City Hall Plaza. The actual crew departure location is not established.
- Preferred plant scale: 2,000–5,000 pigs per week.
- Approximate animal size: 140–220 pounds live weight.
- Products: pelvic, abdominal/bariatric, thoracic and other tissue blocks.
- Possible arrangements: space for the user's harvesting crew or supplier-performed harvesting with training.
- Existing suppliers identified by the user: Parks, Custom Quality Packers, Martin's, Nahunta and Villari.

The thirty contacts comprise **20 new plant prospects, 5 current suppliers, 3 sourcing businesses and 2 referral organizations**. They are not thirty confirmed, fully qualified new suppliers. No new prospect is confirmed to meet the volume, animal, workspace and commercial requirements together.

No supplier calls, messages, visits or pilot collections were performed. The call tracker remains blank for results. Current weekly pig throughput and room availability are unconfirmed where explicitly indicated.
