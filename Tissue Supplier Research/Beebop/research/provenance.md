# Research provenance and limitations

Snapshot date: September 28, 2026.

## Inputs

The baseline is the original workbook supplied in the ChatGPT conversation, preserved in `reference/Tissue_Supplier_Workbook.xlsx`. The new call-plan workbook was created separately. The neighboring repository workbook, `Tissue_Supplier_Study.xlsx`, was not used as a substitute baseline or edited during this upload.

Sources include company websites, the North Carolina Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services plant directory dated September 18, 2026, federal establishment pages where accessible, secondary directories based on Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS) records, and public social information. Federal identification and state-program identification are retained as observed; state directory codes beginning with TA refer to the Talmadge-Aiken program.

Some federal identities were checked through secondary FSIS-derived directories rather than a fresh primary federal download. Links and evidence limitations are retained with each record. An inspection listing does not establish current weekly pig throughput, available room or willingness to host a collection crew.

## Distances

The planning origin is Durham City Hall at 101 City Hall Plaza, Durham, North Carolina, with coordinates 35.99606867413, -78.899683775957. Address matches came primarily from the United States Census Bureau geocoder; specific fallback matches are identified in the records. Bass Farms uses the map pin linked from the company's own website.

Road estimates use Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM) car routes. Miles equal returned route meters divided by 1,609.344. Times are routing estimates, without live traffic. Radial miles use the same origin and destination coordinates. Each contact carries the source address, geocoding evidence and route link.

Distances are planning estimates rather than verified crew travel from an actual workplace. Select Meats and Cruse are near the 120-road-mile boundary. Martin's address match uses a Godwin Street alias for the listed King Street address. Homestead's address is a mobile operator's business base. Sourcing/referral addresses are contact locations, not verified supplying slaughter sites.

## Volume and ranking

Current weekly pig kills are generally not publicly available. Historical or baseline-workbook numbers remain attributed and unconfirmed. Inspection volume categories have not been converted into maximum weekly capacity. Actual throughput, sustainable capacity and the number of animals in the target live-weight range are distinct fields in the call tracker.

The ranking represents the analyst's preferred order of qualification calls, not a numerical eligibility score or a confirmed capacity match. The first ten are new plant prospects. Existing suppliers and referral contacts are identified separately.

## Social information

The twelve social entries cover eight businesses. They include Facebook, Instagram, Nextdoor, and mirrored social posts linking to YouTube. Access was often limited to public search-index excerpts; some full pages or videos were inaccessible. Post dates, where available, are distinct from the research date. A source described as an indexed excerpt was not fully viewed or audited.

Identity matches use the establishment name, address, telephone, official website links and inspection references. Some are partial and explicitly described as such. A successful identity match connects a claim to the business; it does not independently verify the claim. No reviewed social signal establishes the preferred 2,000–5,000-pig weekly throughput or a room-rental offer.

## Relationship to earlier repository work

The existing project README and opening synthesis addenda were read for context before adding this folder. Those documents contain an earlier Pikeville distance origin and broader capacity tiers. This snapshot uses the Durham requirement and approximate live weights stated in the current conversation. Earlier repository documents were not changed or silently reconciled.

The current snapshot contains no fresh legal or export-eligibility determination. Historical claims in the original supplied workbook remain historical source content. No inference of willingness, spare space, tissue suitability or confirmed qualification is drawn solely from inspection status, a retail listing, social activity or facility size.
