What to say when you call / submit the quote

Lead with these so you get commercial-grade material and the right salt form on the first pass.

Exact material & CAS. FB 28: “disodium salt, 4193-55-9” (reject free acid 4404-43-7). BaSO₄: “precipitated/blanc fixe, 7727-43-7, 98–99%”.
Use. “For commercial production, not R&D” — so you’re not sold restricted/exemption material.
Quantity. Ask for 1 kg or a qualification sample; ask if they’ll break a bag.
Form & grade. Industrial/technical (not reagent); powder vs solution (FB 28); particle size/whiteness (BaSO₄).
COA. Confirming purity, salt form (FB 28), particle size (BaSO₄).
Logistics. Delivered US price, lead time, and TSCA status.
