⚠ Read first — pin the exact identity on your RFQ, or you’ll be quoted the wrong material

Fluorescent Brightener 28 trades under two CAS numbers. Your reference sample is the disodium salt:

CASFormWhat to know
4193-55-9Disodium saltThis is your reference material (Sigma 910090). Water-soluble; the standard commercial optical-brightener form — specify this.
4404-43-7Free acidMany listings use this number but ship the disodium salt as ~99% powder. Ask the supplier to confirm salt form on the COA.

“CBS / CBS-X” is NOT FB 28 — it’s a different molecule (CAS 27344-41-8). Many “detergent optical brightener” listings default to CBS-X; don’t accept it as a substitute unless you intend to.

Barium Sulfate — one CAS (7727-43-7), two grades: precipitated “blanc fixe” (synthetic, 98–99%, the production match for an “Extra Pure” lab sample — specify this) vs. natural barite (ground mineral, lower purity, cheaper).

MOQ & pricing at production scale

  • China factory-direct (FOB): ~$400–600/MT for 98–99% precipitated in 25 kg bags. Factory MOQ ~20–25 MT (≈1 FCL); resellers from 1 MT. Lowest-cost path.
  • India: ~₹47–75/kg (~$0.55–0.90/kg), small MOQs — good for trials.
  • Western/Japanese branded (Solvay, Venator, Cimbar BF, Sakai): premium, sold by pallet/bulk bag/truckload; best purity + full documentation.
  • US distributors (Silver Fern, Kish, Basstech, Brenntag): smaller MOQ, domestic logistics — practical for US production without importing an FCL.

Avoid for production — R&D / lab-reagent packaging

Sigma-Aldrich 202762 (“99.99% trace metals”), Fisher / Acros AC222510000 (your reference), LabPro, Stanford Advanced Materials — small-pack R&D grades.

RFQ checklist — paste into your inquiry

Getting these seven fields into the first message avoids R&D grades and back-and-forth.

Exact material & CAS. FB 28: disodium salt, 4193-55-9. BaSO₄: precipitated/blanc fixe, 7727-43-7, 98–99%.
Form & spec. Powder vs solution (FB 28); particle size / whiteness / purity (BaSO₄).
COA showing purity, salt form (FB 28), particle size (BaSO₄).
Quantity tiers. Price at 25 kg / 500 kg / 1 MT (and 20–25 MT for BaSO₄ FCL).
Packaging. 25 kg bags/drums vs 1 MT bulk bags.
Terms. FOB/CIF, lead time, payment (T/T or L/C), TSCA (US) / REACH (EU) status.
Confirm commercial-production use (not an R&D exemption) so there are no use restrictions.
