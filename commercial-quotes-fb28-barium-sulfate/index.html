Getting ≤ 1 kg without the R&D restriction

You need ≤ 1 kg of each, but as commercial / production-grade material — not the R&D-exemption lab reagent your reference SDS sheets carry. At this scale the bulk suppliers matter less; what matters is buying a small commercial pack, a 1 kg trial, or a sample of the real production grade.

Materials: Fluorescent Brightener 28 · Barium Sulfate Target qty: ≤ 1 kg each Replacing: Sigma 910090 & Fisher/Acros AC222510000 (R&D / lab)

⚠ Pin the identity — and know why 1 kg is the tricky part

Fluorescent Brightener 28 — you need the (di)sodium salt, CAS 4193-55-9, NOT the free acid (4404-43-7). Many listings default to the free acid or don’t say; state “disodium salt, 4193-55-9” and require a COA confirming the salt form. “CBS-X” is a different molecule — not a substitute.

Barium Sulfate — specify precipitated “blanc fixe” 98–99% (CAS 7727-43-7), not natural barite.

Avoid at 1 kg — R&D / lab-reagent SKUs

What to say when you order / request a sample

At 1 kg the goal is commercial-grade, unrestricted material — make that explicit up front.

Exact material & CAS. FB 28: disodium salt, 4193-55-9. BaSO₄: precipitated/blanc fixe, 7727-43-7, 98–99%.
State the use. “For commercial production, not R&D” — so you’re not sold restricted/exemption material.
Quantity. Ask for 1 kg (or a sample); ask if they’ll break a bag/drum.
Grade & form. Industrial/technical (not reagent); powder vs solution (FB 28); particle size/whiteness (BaSO₄).
COA confirming purity, salt form (FB 28), particle size (BaSO₄).
Logistics. Price incl. shipping to your country, lead time, and TSCA (US) / REACH (EU) status.
