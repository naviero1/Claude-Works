---

## ⚠️ Read first — get the chemical identity right on your RFQ

**Fluorescent Brightener 28** trades under two CAS numbers — using the wrong one will get you the wrong material:

| CAS | Form | Notes |
|-----|------|-------|
| **4193-55-9** | **Disodium salt** | **This is what your reference sample (Sigma 910090) actually is.** Water-soluble; the standard commercial optical-brightener form. **Specify this on your RFQ.** |
| 4404-43-7 | Free acid | Many Chinese/US listings use this number, but usually ship the disodium salt as ~99% powder. Ask the supplier to confirm salt form on the COA. |

- **"CBS / CBS-X" is NOT Fluorescent Brightener 28.** CBS-X is a different molecule (distyryl-biphenyl-disulfonate, CAS 27344-41-8). Many "detergent optical brightener" listings default to CBS-X, DMS, or CXT — don't accept those as FB 28 unless you intend a substitute.
- Trade names for genuine FB 28: **Calcofluor White M2R, Tinopal UNPA-GX, Blankophor, C.I. Fluorescent Brightener 28 (C.I. 40622)** — a DAST/triazinyl-aminostilbene brightener.

**Barium Sulfate** — one CAS (**7727-43-7**) but two very different grades:
- **Precipitated barium sulfate ("blanc fixe")** — synthetic, high purity (98–99%+), fine/uniform particle. **This is the production match for an "Extra Pure" lab sample.** Specify this.
- **Natural barite / barytes** — ground mineral, lower purity, cheaper (fillers, drilling, radiation shielding). Only if that's what you actually want.

When you RFQ, always ask for: **COA (purity / salt form / particle size), price at your target quantity, packaging, FOB/CIF terms, lead time, and TSCA/REACH import status.**

### Barium sulfate — realistic MOQ & pricing at production scale
- **China factory-direct (FOB):** ~US **$400–600/metric ton** for 98–99% precipitated in 25 kg bags. Factory MOQ often **20–25 MT (≈1 FCL)**; marketplace resellers list from **1 MT**. Lowest-cost path.
- **India (IndiaMART):** ~₹47–75/kg (~$0.55–0.90/kg), small MOQs — good for trials.
- **Western/Japanese branded (Solvay, Venator, Cimbar BF, Sakai):** premium price, sold by pallet / bulk bag / truckload, "contact for quote." Highest, most consistent purity + full documentation — best if "Extra Pure" consistency matters more than price.
- **US distributors (Silver Fern, Kish, Basstech, Brenntag):** smaller MOQ, domestic logistics — the practical choice for US production without importing an FCL.

### Barium sulfate — AVOID for production (R&D / lab-reagent packaging)
Sigma-Aldrich 202762 ("99.99% trace metals"), **Fisher / Acros AC222510000** (your reference), LabPro, Stanford Advanced Materials — small-pack R&D grades.

## RFQ checklist (paste into your inquiry)
1. **Exact material & CAS** — FB 28: "disodium salt, **CAS 4193-55-9**"; Barium sulfate: "**precipitated / blanc fixe**, CAS 7727-43-7, 98–99%."
2. **Form & spec** — powder vs solution (FB 28); particle size / whiteness / purity (BaSO₄).
3. **COA** showing purity, salt form (FB 28), particle size (BaSO₄).
4. **Quantity tiers** — price at e.g. 25 kg / 500 kg / 1 MT (and 20–25 MT for BaSO₄ FCL).
5. **Packaging** — 25 kg bags/drums vs 1 MT bulk bags.
6. **Terms** — FOB/CIF, lead time, payment (T/T or L/C), and **TSCA (US) / REACH (EU) import status**.
7. **Confirm it is for commercial production** (not R&D exemption) so there are no use restrictions.
