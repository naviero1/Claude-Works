## What to say when you call / submit the quote
1. **Exact material & CAS** — FB 28: "disodium salt, 4193-55-9" (reject free acid 4404-43-7). BaSO₄: "precipitated/blanc fixe, 7727-43-7, 98–99%".
2. **Use** — "for commercial production, not R&D" (avoids restricted/exemption material).
3. **Quantity** — 1 kg or a qualification sample; ask if they'll break a bag.
4. **Form & grade** — industrial/technical (not reagent); powder vs solution (FB 28); particle size/whiteness (BaSO₄).
5. **COA** — confirming purity, salt form (FB 28), particle size (BaSO₄).
6. **Logistics** — delivered US price, lead time, TSCA status.
