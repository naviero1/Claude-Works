## ⚠️ Identity — get this exactly right on every RFQ

**Fluorescent Brightener 28** — order the **(di)sodium salt, CAS 4193-55-9** (yellow-tan powder or ~25% aqueous solution; formula C₄₀H₄₂N₁₂Na₂O₁₀S₂, MW 960.94). **Do NOT accept the free acid, CAS 4404-43-7.** Many supplier pages list the free-acid CAS or don't specify — always state "disodium salt, 4193-55-9" and require a **COA confirming the salt form**. Also: **"CBS-X" is a different molecule** (27344-41-8) — not a substitute.

**Barium Sulfate** — order **precipitated / blanc fixe, 98–99%**, not natural ground barite.

### Why 1 kg is the tricky part
At ≤ 1 kg, most sellers you'll find first are **reagent catalogs** (Sigma, Fisher, Spectrum) — the R&D-framed grade you're avoiding. And precipitated barium sulfate is normally a **bulk commodity** (many makers won't sell below a 1000 kg pallet). So the winning approach is one of three routes:

| Route | What it is | Best when |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| **A — Buy a small commercial pack** | Off-the-shelf industrial grade, unrestricted, ships now | You just need material in hand fast |
| **B — 1 kg trial from a manufacturer** | Industrial maker quotes a 1 kg trial of their production grade | You want lowest cost/kg and a named factory |
| **C — Sample the real production grade** | Free/nominal sample of a named COTS grade (Solvay, Cimbar, 3V Sigma…) | You need the exact production material for qualification |

Manufacturer **samples (Route C) are the production material itself** — usually free or nominal for qualification. That's often the cleanest way to get ≤ 1 kg of true COTS grade.

## What to say when you order / request a sample
1. **Exact material & CAS** — FB 28: "**disodium salt, 4193-55-9**" (not free acid). BaSO₄: "**precipitated/blanc fixe, 7727-43-7, 98–99%**".
2. **State the use** — "for **commercial production**, not R&D" — so you aren't sold restricted/exemption material.
3. **Quantity** — ask for **1 kg** (or a sample); ask if they'll break a bag/drum.
4. **Grade & form** — industrial/technical (not reagent); powder vs solution (FB 28); particle size/whiteness (BaSO₄).
5. **COA** — confirming purity, **salt form** (FB 28), particle size (BaSO₄).
6. **Logistics** — price incl. shipping to your country, lead time, **TSCA (US) / REACH (EU)** status.
