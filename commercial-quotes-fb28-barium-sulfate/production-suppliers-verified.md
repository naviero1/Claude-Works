## Guardrails to carry into every RFQ
1. **FB 28:** "disodium salt, CAS **4193-55-9**" (reject free acid 4404-43-7 and CBS-X 27344-41-8); require COA showing salt form.
2. **Proxel:** genuine Arxada = Barentz/Azelis; or a named ~20% BIT drop-in (LANXESS/Clariant) — specify DPG carrier if matching BD20 exactly.
3. **Barium sulfate:** "precipitated/synthetic 98-99%", not natural barite.
4. State **commercial-production use (not R&D)** so you're not sold restricted material.
5. Small quantity = ask for a **qualification sample** where no ~1 kg pack is published.
